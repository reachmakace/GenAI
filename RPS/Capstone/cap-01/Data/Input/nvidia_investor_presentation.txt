NVIDIA Investor Presentation

1. Company Overview

NVIDIA Corporation is a global leader in accelerated computing, known
for its pioneering work in graphics processing units (GPUs), artificial
intelligence (AI), and data center technologies. Founded in 1993, the
company has transitioned from a gaming-focused graphics company to a
dominant player in AI infrastructure, autonomous systems, and enterprise
computing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. Key Business Segments

2.1 Data Center

-   Largest and fastest-growing segment.
-   Driven by demand for AI training and inference workloads.
-   Flagship products include NVIDIA H100, A100, and the upcoming
    next-gen platforms.
-   Powers hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud), research
    institutions, and enterprises.

2.2 Gaming

-   Home of GeForce GPUs.
-   Includes laptops, desktops, and cloud gaming (GeForce NOW).
-   Ray tracing and DLSS (Deep Learning Super Sampling) continue to grow
    adoption.

2.3 Professional Visualization

-   Used in design, engineering, and simulation.
-   Powered by RTX technology.

2.4 Automotive

-   Focus on autonomous driving and AI-based cockpit systems.
-   Includes NVIDIA DRIVE platform.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. Financial Highlights

-   Revenue growth primarily driven by AI and data center.
-   Strong margins due to high-performance hardware and software
    ecosystem.
-   Increasing software-related recurring revenue through CUDA,
    enterprise AI licenses, and Omniverse.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. NVIDIA AI Platform

4.1 CUDA Ecosystem

-   Proprietary parallel computing platform.
-   Foundation for AI training and simulation worldwide.

4.2 DGX Systems

-   Full-stack AI supercomputers.
-   Used extensively for LLM training and enterprise AI adoption.

4.3 Networking (Mellanox)

-   High-speed networking such as InfiniBand.
-   Critical for AI clusters.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

5. Market Opportunities

5.1 Generative AI

-   Massive demand for training LLMs and deploying inference workloads.
-   Enterprise AI adoption increasing globally.

5.2 Autonomous Vehicles

-   Long-term opportunity in AI-powered transportation.

5.3 Industrial Digital Twins (Omniverse)

-   Simulation and robotics acceleration.

5.4 Cloud & Hyperscaler Partnerships

-   Strong collaboration pipeline across major cloud service providers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

6. Strategic Advantages

-   Technological leadership in GPU and AI acceleration.
-   Deep software ecosystem (CUDA, TensorRT, NeMo).
-   Strong partnerships across cloud, automotive, robotics, and
    enterprise.
-   Unique end-to-end platform: hardware + software + networking.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

7. Risks & Challenges

-   Competitive pressure from AMD, Intel, and custom AI chips.
-   Supply chain and manufacturing dependency (primarily TSMC).
-   Regulatory concerns in sensitive markets.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

8. Future Outlook

-   Expected continued leadership in AI infrastructure.
-   Rapid expansion in enterprise AI deployments.
-   Growth of AI PCs and edge AI markets.
-   Strong roadmap for next-generation GPUs, networking, and software
    platforms.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

9. Conclusion

NVIDIA remains positioned as a top beneficiary of the global AI wave.
Its full-stack approach, hardware dominance, and software ecosystem
provide sustained competitive advantages and long-term growth potential.
