Machine learning (ML) model pipelining is the process of organizing the entire ML workflow—from raw data to predictions—into a structured sequence of steps that can be executed automatically and consistently. 

Key points:

- Automates the end-to-end ML workflow.
- Ensures consistency in data preprocessing and feature engineering.
- Makes experiments repeatable and comparable.
- Simplifies deployment and maintenance.
- Encourages modular, clean, and reusable code.
